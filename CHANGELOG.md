# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- Built-in demo source, so the app can be tried without a camera, and a welcome screen on first start.
- Send feedback: opens your email app with a report (device, version, last crash, app log). Camera
  addresses, passwords and stream keys are removed; nothing is sent until you press Send.
- Recording stops on low battery (limit of your choice, only when not charging), when the phone gets too
  hot, or when storage runs low, with a notification.
- Start screen, Camora colours, tab icons, a pulsing REC timer and frame rate on each camera.
- Stop recording from the notification; launcher shortcuts and a quick settings tile.
- QR code and share button for the stream address.
- About section with the website, source code, privacy policy and ways to support the project.
- Right-to-left fixes for file names and sizes.

## 0.3.0 (first public version)

- Watch, record and stream RTSP/ONVIF IP cameras and USB HDMI capture cards (UVC) with sound.
- Motion detection with alerts, also in the background and with the screen off; record on motion.
- Streaming to browsers (low delay), DLNA TVs (live MPEG-TS), Chromecast and HLS players; optional HTTPS
  and opt-in remote access.
- Up to 4K, quality presets, H.264 or H.265 per camera.
- Accurate lip-sync for RTSP cameras.
- Storage management: cleanup by age or size, save to the gallery.
- 8 languages, following the system language.
